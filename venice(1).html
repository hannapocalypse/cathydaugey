La Bauta · Venice

Venetian Carnival

In 2024–25, Cathy trained inside La Bauta, a historic Venetian atelier producing costumes for the Carnival of Venice — arriving without a word of Italian, and leaving with a B1 certification picked up entirely on the job. She helped create, sell, and rent elaborate historical dress, and spent the Carnival season itself dressing customers from all over the world by hand, an immersive introduction to costume as something lived in, not just made.
