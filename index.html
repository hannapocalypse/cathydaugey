About

Every seam is a decision.

Trained between Bègles, Agen, and the ateliers of Venice, Cathy builds costumes the old way — by hand, by fitting, by patience. She has dressed dancers for Giselle and The Nutcracker at the Opéra National de Bordeaux, restored historical carnival dress at La Bauta in Venice, and now works full-time as an assistant costumière at La Malle aux Costumes.

She is quietly obsessed with old castles, candlelight, and anything with a hidden seam.

— C.D.
