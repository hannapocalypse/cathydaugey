Before her professional placements, Cathy trained at Lycée Vaclav Havel and Lycée Antoine Lomet, earning a silver medal at the regional and departmental rounds of the "Meilleure Apprentie de France" competition in 2021. These pieces — a Premier Empire–style gown and a caduceus-emblem coat — were built during that training, in 2024.
